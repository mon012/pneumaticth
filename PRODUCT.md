# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, confirmed by the user. The finished site is a static rebuild of the exported WordPress site.

## Users

Thai engineering, maintenance, purchasing, and factory personnel evaluating pneumatic, hydraulic, industrial filtration, automation, and related engineering information and services.

## Product Purpose

Present the company, its engineering products and services, and its technical knowledge clearly. The primary conversion is clicking through to LINE.

## Positioning

A Thai industrial engineering supplier that combines product/service information with practical technical knowledge for factory teams.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare industrial solutions, read technical articles, inspect supporting images, videos, and PDFs, and contact the company through LINE.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing factual copy.
- Preserve every retained link target exactly because conversion tracking is already configured.
- Preserve outbound links and video/PDF embeds without omissions.
- Remove unused ecommerce surfaces and functionality, including cart, checkout, account, and shop.
- Copy only assets actually referenced by the retained site.
- Preserve retained public URL paths where possible.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: บริษัท นิวเมติก ดอทคอม (ประเทศไทย) จำกัด
- Preserve the existing logo, company imagery, contact details, and factual language.
- LINE is the primary call to action; its existing URL must not be changed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Static WordPress export under `../old wp/` containing the original copy, links, metadata, images, one PDF, and video embeds.
- Existing visual assets under `../old wp/wp-content/uploads/`.
- No new commercial claims, testimonials, pricing, or performance benchmarks may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the path to LINE immediate and unmistakable.
- Earn trust through clear engineering information and real company evidence.
- Preserve content and tracking fidelity during migration.
- Keep the static output lightweight by shipping only referenced assets.
- Make long technical content easy to read on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Thai text must remain readable without truncation or encoding changes.
